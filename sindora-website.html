Policies

Clear terms, no fine-print surprises

The short version of every policy that matters, written in plain language.

Terms & Conditions

By engaging Sindora for any product or service, you agree to the description, scope, pricing and delivery terms shared and agreed at the time of order.

  • You agree to provide accurate information and content needed to complete the work.
  • Custom work is scoped and priced based on what is agreed before the project begins.
  • Requests outside the agreed scope may require additional time and payment.
Privacy Policy

Sindora collects information such as your name, contact details and project requirements to communicate with you, process orders and deliver services and support.

  • Information is used only for legitimate business purposes related to your order or enquiry.
  • Reasonable technical and organisational security measures are used to protect your information.
  • Payment information, where applicable, is handled by third-party payment providers and is not stored by Sindora directly.
Payment Policy

Payment is required according to the price displayed or otherwise agreed with Sindora for your product or service.

  • Custom development begins once the agreed advance, or full payment, has been received.
  • Work outside the originally agreed scope may require a separate, additional payment before it begins.
Refund & Cancellation Policy

Custom projects

This applies to websites, custom digital tools, apps and QR restaurant automation. You may cancel within 2 days of payment, while the project is still ongoing, and receive a full refund, subject to applicable terms and law.

Custom project? You have a 2-day window to cancel for a full refund, as long as the project hasn't been completed.

Direct digital products

Downloadable digital products, direct-access tools and similar resources are non-refundable once payment is made, because access or delivery may be immediate.

Direct digital products and instant-access tools cannot be refunded after payment — please review before purchasing.
Delivery & Project Tracking

For custom websites, digital tools, apps and QR restaurant automation, you get access to a live project tracking dashboard after payment, showing:

  • Current project status
  • Estimated delivery time
  • Final delivery time, once confirmed

Delivery estimates can shift due to project complexity, content you provide, approval turnaround, requested changes or changes to scope. Reasonable revisions within the agreed scope are included where applicable; major changes outside that scope may require additional payment.

Third-Party Services

Projects may depend on third-party providers such as hosting, domain registrars, payment gateways, messaging platforms and other cloud services. Their availability, pricing and policies are controlled by those providers, not Sindora.
